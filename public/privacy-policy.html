1. About this policy

RoomGrub is a shared-expense tracker for people who live together. You create a room, invite your roommates, log what each person spends, and RoomGrub works out who owes whom. Broccly ("we", "us") runs RoomGrub.

This policy covers the RoomGrub website and web app, the RoomGrub Android app, and the backend services behind them.

2. Information we collect

Account information (from Google Sign-In)

When you sign in with Google, Google sends us your name, email address and profile photo URL. We store these to create your account and update them each time you sign in. We never see or store your Google password.

Information you add to RoomGrub

  • Rooms and membership: the rooms you create or join, your role in each (admin or member), and when you joined.
  • Expenses: each expense's description (for example "Rice 5kg"), amount, date, who paid, and who it is split between.
  • Balances and settlements: each member's running balance and a history of past settle-ups.
  • Invites: invite links you create, who created them, and whether they were accepted, rejected or expired.

Device and session information

  • Push notification token (Android app): a Firebase Cloud Messaging token that lets us send notifications to your device, plus the platform it belongs to.
  • Sign-in sessions: a record of each signed-in session, including when it expires, so you stay signed in. We store only a one-way hash of each session token, never the token itself.
  • Technical logs: our servers and hosting providers may keep standard request logs (such as IP address, time, and the address requested) for security and troubleshooting.

What we do not collect

RoomGrub does not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, phone number, or payment details. Settlements happen outside the app, and RoomGrub only records that they happened. We do not use analytics, advertising, or cross-app tracking SDKs.

3. How we use your information

  • To create your account and keep you signed in.
  • To run the core features: rooms, invites, expense tracking, splits, balances and settle-ups.
  • To show room members who added what, and who owes whom.
  • To send the emails and push notifications described below.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.

We do not use your information for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell it.

4. What other members can see

RoomGrub is built for sharing within a room. When you join a room, its other members can see:

  • your name, email address and profile photo;
  • your role in the room;
  • the expenses you paid for or are part of, and your balance and settlement history in that room.

Room members cannot see your other rooms. Anyone who has a room's invite link can use it to join that room until it is used or expires, so share invite links only with people you trust.

5. Emails and notifications

Emails

  • Welcome email: sent to your email address when you first sign up.
  • Settle-up summary: when a room admin settles all expenses, each member gets a summary email listing the members' names, email addresses and balances, and who pays whom.

Push notifications (Android app)

When someone adds an expense that includes you, we send a push notification with their name, the item, the amount and your share. You can turn off notifications at any time in your device's settings for RoomGrub.

7. Cookies and on-device storage

Website

We use only the cookies needed to keep you signed in. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

CookiePurpose

The web app also installs a service worker that caches app files so pages load faster and work offline.

Android app

The app keeps your sign-in tokens in the device's encrypted secure storage. It also caches room and expense data on your device so it opens quickly and works offline. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes all of this.

8. When we share information

We share your information only:

  • with members of the rooms you belong to, as described above;
  • with the service providers listed above, to run RoomGrub;
  • if the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users or the public;
  • if we are involved in a merger or acquisition, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

We never sell or rent your personal information.

9. How long we keep data

  • Account and room data: kept for as long as your account exists.
  • Sign-in sessions: a session ends when you sign out, or after 90 days without use.
  • Invites: expire after 7 days.
  • Deleted rooms: when an admin deletes a room, we delete its expenses, invites and memberships.
  • Leaving a room: expenses you were part of stay in that room's records, so the other members' balances stay correct.
  • Push tokens: deleted with your account. If another account signs in on the same device, the token moves to that account.

10. Security

  • All traffic between the apps and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Access tokens expire after 15 minutes. Session tokens are replaced on every renewal, stored only as one-way hashes, and are revoked when you sign out.
  • On the website, access tokens are never stored in your browser.
  • Every request is checked so you can only see the rooms you belong to, and only admins can change or delete room data.

No method of storing or sending data over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction: your name and photo come from your Google account. Change them there and RoomGrub updates the next time you sign in. Ask a room admin to fix expense entries.
  • Leave a room: members can leave a room at any time. You won't be added to new expenses there, but past shared records stay (see section 9).
  • Notifications: turn push notifications off in your device settings.
  • Sign out: signing out ends your session on that device.
  • Delete your account: email us from the address linked to your account. We will delete your account, profile and push tokens, and remove or anonymize your personal details in shared room records, within 30 days, and confirm by email.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights, such as getting a copy of your data or objecting to certain uses of it. To use any of these rights, contact us.

12. Children's privacy

RoomGrub is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you think a child has given us their information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as RoomGrub changes. We will post every update on this page and change the "Last updated" date. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app or by email.
